Photography — natural is the default

Real, true-to-colour, natural-light photography you can actually shoot. Colour lives in the layout — tags, buttons, headers — not in the photo. This is the everyday standard for the whole site.

Optional treatment — “spotlight duotone”
→ →

A repeatable one-click recipe: desaturate, then tint into the discipline's shade. Reserve it for category banners and hero panels only — one image at a time, never the whole gallery.

✓ Do — keep class galleries and everyday photos natural; let the UI carry the colour.
✗ Don't — recolour every image; it isn't reproducible and dates fast.
