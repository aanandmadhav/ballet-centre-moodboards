Four decades of performing arts, one trusted home.

Founded in Dubai as the UAE's very first dance school, The Ballet Centre has spent forty years nurturing students into confident, world-class performers. Ballet is where it began — but today, one enrolment opens the door to six disciplines and a whole community.
