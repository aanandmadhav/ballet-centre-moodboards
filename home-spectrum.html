Since 1985

Forty years of performing arts, one trusted home.

Founded in Dubai as the UAE's very first dance school, The Ballet Centre has spent four decades nurturing students into confident, world-class performers. Ballet is where it began — today, one enrolment opens the door to six disciplines and a whole community.
