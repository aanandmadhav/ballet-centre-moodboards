The idea

Not an activity centre — a season. Every discipline is an act with its own colour.

Forty years of curtain calls, reframed as a performing-arts programme. Bold, editorial, theatrical. The colour system does the work: pick an act and its colour follows you through the whole site, from the hero to the timetable to the enrol button.
