A heritage direction. Ivory light, a classic serif voice, the brand gradient kept as a quiet accent. Prestige earned over forty years.

The idea

Forty years is a rare thing. This direction treats the Centre like the institution it is: calm, confident and timeless. Nothing shouts. The gradient from the logo appears only as fine detail, so the photography and the space do the talking.
